# Relaxinic demo assets

- `disc.webp` is a web-sized conversion of the locally prepared scratched CD cutout. `disc-mask.svg` shares its geometry. See the project's `assets/cd-v1/README.md` for provenance and the source image.
- `glitch.mp3` is the public preview of [Digital Glitch_v1.wav by tadaizm](https://freesound.org/people/tadaizm/sounds/458065/), listed as CC0.
- `instrument-serif.ttf` is [Instrument Serif](https://github.com/Instrument/instrument-serif), licensed under the included SIL Open Font License.
- `contact-github.svg` is the [GitHub mark from Primer Octicons](https://github.com/primer/octicons/blob/main/icons/mark-github-24.svg), with its MIT license in `../img/Octicons-LICENSE.txt`.
- `contact-cc98.png` is a monochrome conversion of the [CC98 site icon](https://github.com/ZJU-CC98/Forum/blob/main/dist/static/98icon.ico).
- `contact-lastfm.svg` is the [Last.fm mark from Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/lastdotfm.svg).
